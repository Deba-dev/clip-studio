Clip Studio 0.7.9 picks fewer clips from the lobby and menus, and the window can be dragged again in every window mode. It includes everything from 0.7.8.

## New in this version

- **No more lobby hype clips:** a loud moment now has to happen during real play to become a hype clip. Queue screens, agent select, map loading and the buy phase are left out, even on streams without a visible HUD. Tested on a 3-hour Valorant stream: 98% of in-round moments were kept and half of the lobby moments were dropped.
- **Better ranking:** shouting and loud gunfire count a little less on their own, and laughs in the lobby rank lower than laughs during a round.
- **Drag the window again:** a window size saved while in fullscreen could bring Windowed mode back without a title bar, so it could not be moved. That is fixed. In Borderless and Fullscreen, touch the top edge of the screen and drag the new grip (six dots, left of minimize) to pop Clip Studio out into a normal window.

## Install

Download **ClipStudio-Setup.exe** below and run it. Already on 0.7? Clip Studio offers this update by itself.
