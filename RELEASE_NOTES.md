Clip Studio 0.7.8 works with Zunimi Studio, asks before Esc hides it, and has a new icon. It includes everything from 0.7.7.

## New in this version

- **Works with Zunimi Studio:** Clip Studio now listens to Zunimi Studio the same way it listens to OBS. In Settings > OBS or Zunimi Studio, enter address 127.0.0.1 and the port and password from Zunimi's Settings > Remote. Every recording becomes a stream to clip, and every **Mark moment** you press in Zunimi (menu, hotkey, phone or Stream Deck) becomes a clip.
- **Esc asks first:** Esc now asks "Hide to tray, Quit or Cancel" before hiding the window. Cancel is the default, so pressing Esc twice does nothing. Tick "Don't ask again" or switch it off in Settings.
- **New icon** in the Film suite colours.
