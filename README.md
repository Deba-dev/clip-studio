# Clip Studio

**Stream normally. Get your best clips ready to post.**

Clip Studio is a Windows app for streamers that turns your long streams into short vertical clips for YouTube Shorts,
Instagram Reels and TikTok. When OBS stops recording (or when you paste a YouTube, Twitch or Kick link), it finds your
best moments on your own PC: kills and clutches read from the game itself, laughs, hype and chat spikes. You review the
shortlist, fix a trim or a word, and export 9:16 clips with captions in Hindi, Hinglish or English.

**What it does**
- **Finds the moments:** reads Valorant's HUD for every kill, death and clutch, learns other games' kill notices, and hears laughter and shouting.
- **Edits for you:** punch-in zoom on kills, a hook in the first seconds, kill counters and styled captions.
- **Runs on your PC:** no uploads, no GPU needed, and it waits until your stream has ended.
- **Exports** at 720p to 1440p and up to 60 fps, at phone loudness.
